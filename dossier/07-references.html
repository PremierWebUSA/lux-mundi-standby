About this chapter

This is the reference register for the whole research dossier. Its 244 numbered entries are grouped into four series: context, evidence and the modern period (A), the canonical life and teaching record (L), prophecy and scriptural interpretation (P), and ancient records and religious movements (R). The numbers match the reference links throughout the other chapters.

Each entry gives the citation, a short note on the question for which it is used, and the domain where the source can be consulted. The register is open about its method: a citation of a publisher’s description, catalogue record or abstract does not mean the complete book or every manuscript was inspected.
