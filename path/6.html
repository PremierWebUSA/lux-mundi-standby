Stop VI of XIIc. AD 93

A Jewish historian

Writing in Rome around AD 93, the Jewish historian Josephus described Jesus as a wise man and a doer of startling deeds, condemned by Pilate. Most scholars hold that the authentic core of this passage remains: the testimony of a non-Christian writer of the first century.
