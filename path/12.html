What history can establish

Taken together, these witnesses establish what nearly all historians accept: Jesus of Nazareth lived, gathered followers, and was crucified under Pontius Pilate, and his followers proclaimed that he had risen. History can carry you this far, to the foot of the cross and to the witnesses of an empty tomb. The last step is yours to take.Jesus once asked it of his own disciples, and it is asked of every reader still:

“Who do you say that I am?”Matthew 16:15
