Stop X of XIIc. AD 500

A hostile tradition

The Babylonian Talmud preserves an early rabbinic tradition that “Yeshu” was hanged on the eve of Passover for sorcery and for leading Israel astray. Scholars debate how much it reflects first-century memory, but it never questions that he lived. Instead it disputes the meaning of his works.
