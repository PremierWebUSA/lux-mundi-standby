Codex Alexandrinus: Mark 6:27–54, British Library, London · Public domain
Stop III of XIIc. AD 70

The first Gospel

Around AD 70, within the lifetime of eyewitnesses, Mark set down the first continuous account of Jesus’s ministry, death and empty tomb. Early tradition links it to Peter’s preaching. Matthew and Luke both drew on it, so much of what we know of Jesus passes through this short, urgent book.
