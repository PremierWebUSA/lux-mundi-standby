Stop IX of XIIc. AD 116

Rome’s historian

Tacitus, the greatest Roman historian, disliked the Christians. Writing around AD 116 about the fire of Rome, he explains that their name came from “Christus,” who was executed under Tiberius by the procurator Pontius Pilate. A hostile witness confirms the central fact of the Gospels: the crucifixion under Pilate.
