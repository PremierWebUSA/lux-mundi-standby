Stop I of XIIAD 54

The earliest testimony

Begin with the oldest Christian writing that tells of the resurrection. Paul wrote this letter around AD 54. Here he passes on a creed he had himself received, which most historians date to within a few years of the crucifixion. It names the witnesses who could still be questioned: Peter, the Twelve, James, and more than five hundred others.
