About this place

A settlement on the north-west shore of the Dead Sea, beside the caves where about 900 manuscripts, the Dead Sea Scrolls, were found between 1947 and 1956. Most scholars link the site with a Jewish sectarian community, often identified with the Essenes. The Great Isaiah Scroll from Cave 1 shows the text of Isaiah long before Jesus.

    The Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsaᵃ)

    Unknown Jewish scribe

    The only complete biblical book among the Dead Sea Scrolls, copied in the second century BC. It shows that Isaiah, a book Christians read as foretelling Jesus, existed in substantially its known form well before Christianity.
