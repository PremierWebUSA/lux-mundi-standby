Frankfurt (Nida)

Show on the map
About this place

The Roman town of Nida, in the province of Upper Germany, lies beneath the modern district of Heddernheim in Frankfurt. In 2018 a grave of about AD 230–270 yielded a rolled silver amulet whose Latin text invokes Jesus Christ, read by CT scanning in 2024: one of the earliest Christian witnesses north of the Alps.
