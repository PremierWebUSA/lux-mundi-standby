Tiberius

Who they were

Tiberius Julius Caesar Augustus succeeded Augustus in AD 14 and reigned until 37, spending his last decade on Capri. Pilate governed Judea as his appointee, and the silver denarius shown to Jesus in the question about tribute probably bore his image.

Luke dates John the Baptist’s preaching to the fifteenth year of Tiberius (c. AD 28–29). Tacitus says Christus was executed ‘in the reign of Tiberius’ by Pilate, and Justin Martyr repeats the dating. Later Christian legend, reported by Tertullian and Eusebius, claimed that Tiberius proposed to recognise Christ as a god, a story historians do not accept.
