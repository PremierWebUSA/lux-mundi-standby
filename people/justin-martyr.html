Justin Martyr

See them in the chain of witnesses
Who they were

Justin was born at Flavia Neapolis (Nablus) in Samaria, studied several philosophies, became a Christian, and taught in Rome, where he was beheaded around 165 with several of his students. His First Apology, addressed to Antoninus Pius around 150–155, defends Christians against condemnation for the name alone.

He gives the earliest detailed description of Christian Sunday worship and baptism, refers to the Gospels as the apostles’ ‘memoirs’, and argues at length that the Hebrew prophets foretold Jesus. He appended Hadrian’s rescript to his Apology. Tatian was his pupil.
