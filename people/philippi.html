About this place

A Roman colony in eastern Macedonia, the first city in Europe where, according to Acts, Paul founded a church. His warm letter to the Philippians contains the early hymn of Christ who ‘emptied himself’. 2 Corinthians is thought to have been written from Macedonia, traditionally from Philippi.

    Paul’s Second Letter to the Corinthians

    Paul

    Paul’s second surviving letter to Corinth, c. AD 55–56, written in a season of strain. It defends his apostleship and recalls his escape from Damascus under King Aretas, an episode that helps anchor Paul’s early chronology.
