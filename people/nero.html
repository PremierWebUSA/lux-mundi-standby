Nero

Who they were

Nero reigned from 54 to AD 68. After the great fire of Rome in July 64, Tacitus says, he deflected rumours of his own guilt by blaming the Christians, whom he had put to death with mockery, some torn by dogs and some burned as torches. Suetonius also mentions punishments of Christians under him.

Christian tradition from 1 Clement and Eusebius onward places the deaths of Peter and Paul in Rome in his reign. Many interpreters connect the number 666 in Revelation with his name, and the legend that he would return haunted later writers.
