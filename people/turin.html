About this place

The city where the Shroud, a linen cloth bearing the image of a crucified man, has been kept since 1578, now in the Cathedral of St John the Baptist. Radiocarbon tests in 1988 dated it to AD 1260–1390; the result has been challenged, and the cloth remains an object of devotion and debate.

    The Shroud of Turin: radiocarbon and later dating claims

    Unknown

    A linen cloth bearing the image of a crucified man, venerated as Jesus’s burial shroud. Three laboratories dated it to about AD 1260–1390 in 1988. Later challenges, including a 2022 X-ray study, have not displaced that result, though the debate continues.
