Alexandria

Show on the map
About this place

The great Greek city of Egypt, a centre of learning and of a large Jewish community. Its Christian catechetical teachers included Clement and Origen, and the Secret Mark letter claims Mark wrote a ‘more spiritual gospel’ for the church there. Its bishop Athanasius issued in 367 the earliest known list of exactly the twenty-seven New Testament books.

  1. AD 367AD 367

    Athanasius of Alexandria, Festal Letter 39

    Athanasius of Alexandria

    The Easter letter of 367 in which the bishop of Alexandria lists the books of Scripture. It is the earliest surviving list of exactly the 27 books of today’s New Testament, and it names other books, including the Didache, as useful but outside the canon.
