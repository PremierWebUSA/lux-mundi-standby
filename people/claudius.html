Claudius

Who they were

Claudius reigned from 41 to AD 54. Suetonius reports that he expelled from Rome the Jews who were constantly causing disturbances ‘at the instigation of Chrestus’, an expulsion usually dated to AD 49 on the authority of the fifth-century historian Orosius.

Acts 18:2 says that Aquila and Priscilla had come to Corinth because Claudius had ordered all Jews to leave Rome. Scholars still debate the identity of Chrestus, but many historians think the disturbances arose from disputes in Rome’s synagogues over claims about Christ, which would make this the earliest pagan reference to the Christian movement.
