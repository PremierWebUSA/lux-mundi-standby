About this place

The river running from the Sea of Galilee to the Dead Sea. John the Baptist immersed people in it, and Jesus was baptized there. The coordinates mark the traditional baptism site near Jericho, at Qasr al-Yahud and Bethany beyond the Jordan; the exact place is not known.
